✦ Frequently Asked

Questions We Get Before Every Project

What does Weston Engineers actually do?

We're a project management consultancy (PMC) and construction company based in New Town, Kolkata. We provide site supervision, quality control, BOQ verification, contractor management, and full turnkey construction — for developers, builders, landowners, architects, institutions, and homeowners across Kolkata and West Bengal.

How much does a PMC service cost in Kolkata?

It depends on project size, duration, and scope — a full PMC engagement on a multi-unit development costs differently than periodic site supervision on a single home. We give a straight quote after understanding your project, not a generic rate card. Contact us for a free project audit.

Do you only work in New Town and Rajarhat?

New Town and Rajarhat are where most of our current projects are, but we take on construction and PMC work across Kolkata and West Bengal more broadly. Tell us your project location and we'll tell you straight whether it's a fit.

Can you supervise a project my own contractor is already building?

Yes — independent site supervision and BOQ verification on a project managed by your own contractor is one of our most common engagements. It gives you an accountable check on quality and billing without replacing the contractor relationship you already have.

Do you handle small renovations, or only large builds?

Both. We take on everything from single-room renovations and structural repairs to full multi-unit residential and commercial construction. The site discipline is the same regardless of scale.

How is your billing verified — do I just have to trust you too?

No. Our own work is billed against the same BOQ discipline we hold contractors to — itemized, measurable, and checkable against what's actually been done on site. Transparent billing is one of the three things we build our reputation on, alongside quality control and on-time delivery.
