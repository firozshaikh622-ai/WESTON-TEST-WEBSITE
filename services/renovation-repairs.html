Home Renovation & Repair Contractor in Kolkata

Structural repairs, roofing, flooring, and finishing work for existing properties across Kolkata — done to last, not just to look finished on handover day.

✦ What This Service Covers

Renovation Without Cutting Corners

Renovation work gets rushed more than new construction — it's smaller, it's assumed to be simple, and it's often priced to move fast. We treat it with the same site discipline as a full build.

Structural Repairs & Finishing

Cracked walls, water seepage, and roof leaks in older Kolkata properties are usually symptoms of a structural issue, not just a cosmetic one. Our repair work starts with identifying the actual cause — drainage, foundation movement, or waterproofing failure — rather than just patching the visible damage.

For finishing work — tile installation, flooring, and interior or exterior painting — we hold the same inspection discipline as our construction projects, so a renovation doesn't become a recurring repair bill.
