Site Works & Excavation Contractor in Kolkata

Excavation, demolition, and road & deck construction — the groundwork stage handled with the same site discipline as our building projects.

✦ What This Service Covers

Groundwork That Sets Up Everything After It

Excavation and demolition happen before there's anything visible to inspect — which is exactly why they get rushed by contractors who know the client can't easily verify the work later.

Excavation, Demolition & Road Construction

For new builds, our excavation work accounts for the actual soil profile of the plot — not a generic depth assumption — and is inspected before foundation work begins on top of it. For demolition on occupied or adjacent-property sites, we plan sequencing to avoid structural risk to neighboring buildings, a real constraint in dense New Town and Rajarhat plots.

We also handle road and deck construction for developments needing internal access roads or outdoor decking, coordinated with the same contractor-management discipline as the rest of the build.
