Service 01 • Project Management & Consultancy

Project Management Consultancy in Kolkata

Independent PMC oversight for developers, builders, landowners, and institutions — one civil engineer accountable for your project from feasibility to handover.

✦ What This Service Covers

Why Projects Need a PMC, Not Just a Contractor

A contractor is paid to build. A project management consultancy is paid to protect you — your budget, your timeline, and your quality standard — independent of who's doing the building.

Civil Engineering Consulting

Before you commit capital to a site, we run independent technical due diligence — soil and structural feasibility, cost benchmarking, and regulatory compliance checks specific to West Bengal municipal norms. Developers and landowners in New Town and Rajarhat bring us in at the feasibility stage precisely because our advice isn't tied to a construction contract we're trying to win.

For projects already underway, our civil engineers provide a second technical opinion on structural drawings, load calculations, and material specifications — catching design issues before they become site issues.

✦ Construction Management

One Point of Accountability, Start to Finish

Most construction delays and cost overruns in Kolkata don't come from bad contractors — they come from nobody owning the coordination between contractors, suppliers, and the client. That's the job we take on.

01

Contractor Coordination

We manage the interface between structural, MEP, and finishing contractors so handoffs don't stall the schedule.

02

Look-Ahead Scheduling

Weekly look-ahead plans that surface material and manpower bottlenecks before they delay the critical path.

03

BOQ & Billing Verification

Every contractor bill is checked against measured quantities on site — not just against the contractor's own claim.
