✦ What This Service Covers

Construction, Run Like a Managed Project

Most builders in Kolkata quote a price and disappear until the finishing stage. We run construction the way we'd want it run if it were our own building — daily site presence, transparent billing, and a schedule you can actually see.

New Building & Custom Home Construction

Whether it's a new residential building in New Town, a custom home in Rajarhat, or an accessory structure on an existing plot, we manage the full build — layout marking, structural work, MEP coordination, and finishing — under one contract with one point of accountability.
