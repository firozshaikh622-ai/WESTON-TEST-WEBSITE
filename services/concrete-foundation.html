Concrete & Foundation Contractor in Kolkata

Slab foundations, concrete pouring, and drainage engineered for West Bengal's soil conditions — verified against BOQ before every payout.

✦ What This Service Covers

The Part of the Build You Can't Fix Later

Foundation mistakes are the most expensive mistakes in construction — they're also the ones a homeowner or developer has the least ability to catch on their own, because the evidence gets buried in weeks.

Foundation Work for New Town & Rajarhat Soil

New Town and Rajarhat sit on reclaimed and low-lying land with soil conditions that punish shortcuts — inadequate drainage or under-specified foundation depth shows up as cracking and settling within a few monsoons, not decades. Our foundation work is specified against actual soil data for the plot, not a generic template.

Every stage — excavation, reinforcement, pouring, and curing — is inspected on site before the next stage begins, and every concrete quantity is checked against the BOQ so you're not paying for material that never went into the ground.
