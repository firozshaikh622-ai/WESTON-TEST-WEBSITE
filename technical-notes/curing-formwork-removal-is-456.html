Curing Periods and Formwork Removal Times (IS 456)

Weston Engineers • Technical Note • Reviewed September 2026

Curing and formwork stripping are where schedules quietly steal strength from a building. Both are cheap to do right and very expensive to get wrong.

Minimum Curing (IS 456, Clause 13.5.1)

ConditionKeep exposed surfaces continuously moist for at least
Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC)7 days
Mineral admixtures or blended cement (e.g. PPC)10 days (extending to 14 days is recommended)
Concrete exposed to dry and hot weather10 days

Most residential work in Kolkata uses PPC, so 10 days is the practical minimum on most of our sites, not the 7 days many crews assume.

Formwork Removal (IS 456, Clause 11.3.1)

Minimum times for OPC concrete at normal temperatures (above about 15 °C), where props are left in place as stated:

MemberMinimum period before striking
Vertical formwork to columns, walls, beams16 – 24 hours
Soffit formwork to slabs (props left under)3 days
Soffit formwork to beams (props left under)7 days
Props to slabs spanning up to 4.5 m7 days
Props to slabs spanning over 4.5 m14 days
Props to beams and arches spanning up to 6 m14 days
Props to beams and arches spanning over 6 m21 days

These periods can change for other cement types, admixtures or cold weather. The engineer’s instruction on site takes priority, and cube results can justify early striking.

What We Check on Site

  • Curing starts as soon as the surface can take it, not the next morning
  • Ponding or wet hessian on slabs, and wet hessian or curing compound on columns
  • A curing log per pour with the date started and the date completed
  • Striking dates recorded on the pour card and approved before props come out
  • No stacking of bricks or materials on young slabs

Reference

  • IS 456:2000, clauses 11.3 (Stripping time) and 13.5 (Curing)

Summary for site use. Always work from the current edition of the standard and your project specification. Where they differ, the structural engineer’s specification governs.
