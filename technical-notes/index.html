Technical Notes

IS Code Technical Notes

Weston Engineers • For owners, engineers and site teams

These notes turn the Indian Standard clauses we use every day on site into plain language: what the code requires, and what we actually check. They are written for owners who want to know what good work looks like, and for engineers and students who want a quick reference.

  • Concrete Cover and Durability: IS 456 Requirements by Exposure
    Nominal cover, minimum cement content, maximum water-cement ratio and minimum grade for RCC by exposure condition, as per IS 456:2000.
  • Concrete Cube Tests: Sampling, Testing and Acceptance (IS 456)
    How many concrete cubes to cast, how they are tested, and how to judge 28-day results against IS 456 acceptance criteria.
  • Slump Test on Site: Procedure and Workability Ranges
    How to do a slump test on site, what the result means, and the workability ranges IS 456 recommends for footings, slabs, columns and piles.
  • Curing Periods and Formwork Removal Times (IS 456)
    Minimum curing periods for OPC and blended cement, and IS 456 minimum times before removing formwork and props from slabs, beams and walls.
  • TMT Bar Grades Explained: Fe415, Fe500, Fe500D and Fe550D (IS 1786)
    What Fe415, Fe500, Fe500D and Fe550D mean under IS 1786, their strength and elongation requirements, and how to check steel on delivery.
  • Sand Grading Zones I to IV (IS 383): What They Mean on Site
    What sand grading zones mean under IS 383, the sieve limits for Zones I to IV, and simple site checks for silt before sand goes into concrete.

See also our pile foundation guide (IS 2911) and our downloadable QA/QC inspection checklist.
