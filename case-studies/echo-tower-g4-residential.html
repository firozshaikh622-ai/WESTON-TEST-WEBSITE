Case Study • Residential PMC

Echo Tower — G+4 Residential Building

Action Area II, New Town, Kolkata • Ongoing

LocationAction Area II, New Town, Kolkata
Project TypeResidential Development (G+4)
DurationOngoing — scheduled for completion before Durga Puja 2026
Weston's RoleProject Management Consultancy (PMC)
Scope of WorkConstruction supervision, quality control, contractor coordination, progress monitoring, technical guidance

The Challenge

Soil conditions at the site required a pile foundation rather than a conventional spread footing — a decision that raises the technical stakes before a single floor of the superstructure can go up. Proper planning, piling execution, and foundation sequencing all had to be right before construction could safely proceed above ground.

Our Approach

Weston's team coordinated the piling operations directly, monitored foundation quality at each stage, and verified that every structural activity followed the approved drawings and engineering standards before authorizing the move to superstructure work.

Outcome

  • Construction progressing as planned
  • Quality maintained throughout execution
  • Budget effectively controlled
  • Simplified project execution for the client
  • Scheduled for timely completion before Durga Puja 2026
