Case Study • Design & Build

G+4 Residential Building — Action Area II-D

Premises No. 01-715, Action Area II-D, New Town • Ongoing

LocationPremises No. 01-715, Action Area II-D, New Town
Project TypeResidential Building (G+4)
Plot SizeApproximately 2.5 Kathas
DurationOngoing — expected handover January–February 2027
Weston's RoleProject Management Consultancy (PMC) & Main Construction Contractor
Scope of WorkComplete A-to-Z construction, structural execution, waterproofing systems, quality control, material management, project planning, site supervision

The Challenge

This project didn't involve any significant site-related obstacles. With a clear site and straightforward conditions, the real challenge was execution discipline — delivering premium-quality construction that met the client's expectations at every stage, not just at handover.

Our Approach

Weston took on both PMC oversight and main construction contracting, which meant one team was accountable end to end. We used high-quality materials, advanced waterproofing systems, and strict quality inspections at every construction stage to achieve the finish the client was looking for.

Outcome

  • Premium-quality residential construction
  • Complete end-to-end execution under one team
  • High client satisfaction
  • Project progressing according to schedule
  • Expected timely handover in early 2027
