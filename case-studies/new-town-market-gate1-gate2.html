Case Study • Public Infrastructure

New Town Market — Gate 1 & Gate 2

Opposite Tata Cancer Hospital, New Town • 1 Year

LocationOpposite Tata Cancer Hospital, New Town
ClientNKDA (New Town Kolkata Development Authority)
ContractorAzad Enterprise
Weston's RoleProject Management Consultancy (PMC)
Duration1 Year
Scope of WorkConstruction supervision, quality control, daily monitoring, contractor coordination, technical inspection, progress reporting

The Challenge

Excavation on this site was particularly difficult because heavy machinery such as JCBs couldn't be used — the working space was too restricted. The excavation had to be carried out manually, with underground soil conditions requiring careful hand-cutting, closer to archaeological-style excavation than a typical construction dig.

Our Approach

Weston planned and executed the entire excavation manually, with continuous supervision to ensure safety, structural accuracy, and construction quality while keeping project progress on track.

Outcome

  • Both market gates completed successfully
  • Excellent construction quality achieved
  • Minor delay due to heavy monsoon rains
  • Project delivered substantially on schedule to NKDA
