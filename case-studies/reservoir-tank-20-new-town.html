Case Study • Infrastructure

Reservoir Tank No. 20

New Town, Kolkata • 2 Years

LocationNew Town, Kolkata
Project Type1,250 Lakh Litres Overhead Water Reservoir
Duration2 Years
Scope of WorkConstruction supervision, quality assurance, site coordination, progress monitoring, contractor management

The Challenge

The project faced continuous local stakeholder issues alongside seasonal rainfall, both of which affected site accessibility and construction activity over the course of a two-year build.

Our Approach

Weston's team maintained close coordination with local stakeholders, managed work sequencing efficiently around access constraints, and adjusted the construction schedule to minimize delays caused by weather and site conditions.

Outcome

  • Successfully completed a 1,250 lakh litre reservoir
  • High construction quality maintained throughout
  • Minor delay of only 1–2 months despite stakeholder and weather challenges
  • Overall project delivered successfully with controlled execution
