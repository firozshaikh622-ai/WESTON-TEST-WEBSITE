✦ Local Context

High-Volume Group Housing Needs Consistent Quality Control

Ghaziabad, regulated by the Ghaziabad Development Authority (GDA) and UP RERA, is one of the NCR's highest-volume residential construction corridors — Indirapuram, Raj Nagar Extension, and Vaishali among them. At that build volume, quality control is often the first thing that slips without independent, dedicated oversight separate from the contractor's own reporting.

We deliver PMC, site supervision, and BOQ verification in Ghaziabad through our on-ground associate network, applying the same independent quality-control discipline we've built serving high-density development in Kolkata.
