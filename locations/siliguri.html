Construction & PMC Services in Siliguri

Independent project management for residential and commercial construction in Siliguri, the gateway to North Bengal, Sikkim, and the Northeast, extending the same site-supervision and QA/QC process built serving New Town, Rajarhat, and Salt Lake.

✦ Local Context

A Trade Hub With Terrain-Specific Building Requirements

Siliguri, under the Siliguri Jalpaiguri Development Authority (SJDA), sits at the base of the Himalayan foothills and functions as the trade and logistics gateway to Sikkim, the Northeast, Nepal, and Bhutan — the Siliguri Corridor. Higher rainfall, proximity to hill terrain, and a growing commercial and warehousing sector mean construction and drainage considerations here differ meaningfully from the plains-based planning norms of New Town or central Kolkata.

We bring the same independent PMC discipline — site supervision, contractor coordination, quality control — to Siliguri projects, with attention to the terrain and drainage factors specific to the region.
