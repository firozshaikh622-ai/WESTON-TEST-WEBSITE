Construction & PMC Services in Asansol

Independent project management and quality control for residential and commercial construction along Asansol's GT Road corridor, extending the same site-supervision and QA/QC process built serving New Town, Rajarhat, and Salt Lake.

✦ Local Context

An Industrial City With Uneven Building Stock

Asansol, West Bengal's second-largest city, sits in the coal and steel industrial belt and is governed by Asansol Municipal Corporation within the ADDA planning area. Its building stock ranges from decades-old industrial-era structures to new residential development along the GT Road corridor — which means every project here starts with a different baseline, and generic contractor oversight often misses that.

We apply the same independent site-supervision and BOQ verification process across Asansol that we use in Kolkata, adjusted for the specific approval and compliance path through Asansol Municipal Corporation and ADDA.
