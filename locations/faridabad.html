✦ Local Context

An Industrial-Residential Mix Along the NH-44 Corridor

Faridabad, governed by Faridabad Municipal Corporation and regulated under Haryana RERA, combines established industrial zones with a growing residential base along the NH-44 corridor. Projects here often sit at the interface of industrial-adjacent construction standards and standard residential building codes, which is a distinction generic contractors frequently miss.

We deliver PMC and site-supervision services in Faridabad through our on-ground associate network, bringing the same independent oversight discipline we apply across our Kolkata and Delhi NCR projects.
