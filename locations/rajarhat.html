✦ Local Context

Home Turf, Not a Service Radius

Our office sits on Road Dash Drone in Rajarhat, which means site visits here aren't a scheduled trip across the city — they're a short drive, so unscheduled checks and same-day response to a site issue are realistic, not aspirational.

Rajarhat spans a wider mix than New Town's planned Action Areas: older residential plots alongside newer development, which means projects here range from ground-up construction to renovation and structural repair on existing buildings. We handle both, and having our base here means we know which contractors, material suppliers, and local approval offices are reliable, because we've already worked with them.
