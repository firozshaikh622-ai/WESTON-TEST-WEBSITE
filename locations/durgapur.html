✦ Local Context

A Planned Industrial Township Now Growing Beyond Its Core

Durgapur was built as a planned township around Durgapur Steel Plant, and falls under the Asansol-Durgapur Development Authority (ADDA). Its original core has well-established infrastructure, but private residential and commercial development along the Durgapur Expressway and City Centre area is growing faster than that original planning framework anticipated — which is exactly where independent construction oversight adds the most value.

For developers and landowners building outside the legacy township zones, we bring the same PMC discipline — contractor coordination, BOQ verification, quality inspection — that we've applied on multi-plot developments in New Town, adapted to ADDA's approval process.
