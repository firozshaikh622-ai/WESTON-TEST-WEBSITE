✦ Local Context

Built on HIDCO-Developed Land, We Know How It's Built

New Town's Action Areas are laid out and regulated by HIDCO and NKDA, which means plot norms, drainage requirements, and approval processes here differ from older parts of Kolkata. We've delivered civil works directly within these Action Areas — a public clock tower installation, market development structures, and water tower construction and completion — so our teams already know the specific compliance and site-access realities of building here, not just the general Kolkata norms.

For landowners and developers on AA I, II, or III plots, that local track record matters more than it sounds: NKDA/HIDCO inspection requirements and utility tie-ins are specific enough that a contractor without New Town experience often loses time re-learning them on your project.
