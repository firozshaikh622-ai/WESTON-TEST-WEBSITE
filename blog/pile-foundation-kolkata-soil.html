Pile Foundations in Kolkata Soil: Why, When and What to Check

By Firoz Shaikh, Founder • Kolkata • 7 min read

On our Echo Tower G+4 project in Action Area II, the soil report ruled out conventional footings and called for a pile foundation. That is common in this part of Kolkata. Here is why, and what has to go right on site, because a pile is the one part of a building you can never inspect again once it is cast.

Why Kolkata Soil Often Needs Piles

  • Deltaic clay. Kolkata sits on the Ganga delta. The upper layers are typically soft to medium silty clay with limited bearing capacity.
  • High water table. Groundwater is close to the surface for much of the year, which weakens shallow soil and complicates open excavation.
  • Low-lying and filled land. Much of eastern Kolkata, including Salt Lake and parts of New Town, was developed on wetlands, low-lying fields or filled ground. Fill can settle unevenly for years.

Where the soil near the surface cannot safely carry the load, piles carry it down to firmer strata. The decision belongs to the structural engineer, based on a proper soil investigation, never on what the building next door used.

The Usual Choice: Bored Cast-in-Situ Piles

For residential and small commercial buildings in Kolkata, bored cast-in-situ concrete piles are the most common type. A hole is bored, kept stable with bentonite slurry, a reinforcement cage is lowered, and concrete is placed from the bottom up through a tremie pipe. They are governed by IS 2911 (Part 1/Section 2):2010. Key requirements include:

RequirementIS 2911 (Part 1/Sec 2):2010
Minimum grade of concreteM25
Minimum cement content400 kg/m³ (not below 350 kg/m³ even with mix design and admixtures)
Slump of tremie concrete at pouring150 – 180 mm
Clear cover to main reinforcementNot less than 50 mm
Bottom slurry before concretingDo not start concreting if its specific gravity exceeds 1.12
Load testingCovered separately in IS 2911 (Part 4)

What We Check on Site, Pile by Pile

  1. Setting out. Pile centres are surveyed against the pile layout drawing before boring starts.
  2. Verticality and depth. The boring is kept plumb and taken to the design founding level, and the strata encountered are recorded.
  3. Borehole cleaning. The bottom is cleaned and the slurry checked before the cage goes in. Concreting doesn’t start with dirty slurry at the base.
  4. Reinforcement cage. We check bar diameter and count, laps, ring spacing and cover blocks against the drawing.
  5. Tremie concreting. The pour is continuous and the tremie pipe stays embedded in the concrete throughout. We check slump at the pour and cast cube samples.
  6. Records. Every pile gets a record of its depth, concrete volume against the theoretical volume, timings and any interruption.
  7. Cut-off and testing. Weak concrete at the top is chipped off to sound concrete at cut-off level. Integrity tests (see IS 14893) and load tests are done as the structural engineer specifies.

What Goes Wrong Without Supervision

  • Short piles, stopped above the design depth to save time
  • Necking or soil inclusions from a collapsed borehole or a tremie pulled out mid-pour
  • Concrete that is too stiff to flow, leaving voids around the cage
  • Cages without cover blocks, touching the soil
  • No record of what was actually built, so nobody can prove the foundation matches the design

None of these can be fixed cheaply after the fact. That is why the foundation stage is where independent supervision matters most. It is also why a quote that assumes an isolated footing without a soil report should worry you. See how foundations affect construction cost.

Common Questions

Why do so many buildings in Kolkata need pile foundations?

Much of Kolkata, especially the eastern side including Salt Lake and parts of New Town, sits on soft deltaic clay, low-lying land or filled ground with a high water table. Where the soil near the surface cannot safely carry the building load, piles transfer it to firmer strata deeper down.

Does every G+4 building in New Town need piles?

No. The soil investigation report decides. Some plots can use isolated or raft footings. Never decide the foundation type from what a neighbouring building used.

What should be checked during piling?

Pile position and verticality, boring depth against the design, bentonite condition and borehole cleaning before concreting, the reinforcement cage and cover, tremie concreting done as one continuous pour, cube samples, and integrity or load testing as specified by the structural engineer.
