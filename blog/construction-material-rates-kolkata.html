Construction Material Rates in Kolkata: Cement, Steel, Sand, Bricks

By Firoz Shaikh, Founder • Kolkata • Last checked 24 September 2026

Material prices move every month, and they are the biggest variable in any construction estimate. Below are current indicative rates for Kolkata, compiled from published market trackers. Use them to sanity-check a quote or a running bill, then confirm with your supplier on the day you order.

Last checked: 24 September 2026. Rates are retail ranges and exclude delivery unless stated. They vary by brand, quantity and location.

Using These Rates Properly

  • Rates are only half the bill. Quantity is the other half. A fair rate on an inflated quantity is still overbilling, which is why running bills should be checked against measured work.
  • Price the specification, not the name. A cheaper brand is fine if it meets the IS grade. An expensive one is wasted if it is stored badly or used past its shelf life.
  • Test on delivery. Check cement batch dates, steel test certificates and sand silt content before material is used.

Common Questions

What is the price of cement in Kolkata right now?

As of August 2026, published retail ranges in Kolkata were about Rs 355 to Rs 380 per 50 kg bag for PPC, Rs 370 to Rs 395 for OPC 43 and Rs 395 to Rs 420 for OPC 53. Prices vary by brand and order size, so confirm with your dealer.

What is the TMT bar rate in Kolkata?

Published retail ranges for Fe500D TMT bars in Kolkata in mid-2026 were roughly Rs 55 to Rs 67 per kg depending on diameter and brand. Trade rates per tonne are lower.

Is river sand or M-sand better?

Both can be used if they meet IS 383 grading and silt limits. River sand is usually preferred for plaster. M-sand must be properly washed and graded. Test the sand, don't choose by name.
