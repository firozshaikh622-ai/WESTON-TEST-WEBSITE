Construction Cost per Sq Ft in Kolkata (2026)

By Firoz Shaikh, Founder • Kolkata • 7 min read • Updated 24 September 2026

“How much per square foot?” is the first question almost every client asks us. It is also the number most likely to mislead you, because a per sq ft rate only means something when you know exactly what specification sits behind it. Here are current Kolkata figures, what they include, and how to check a contractor’s quote.

The Short Answer: 2026 Ranges

These ranges are compiled from cost trackers published in 2026 (listed under Sources). They are per sq ft of built-up area for a residential building, construction only.

SpecificationIndicative cost per sq ftWhat it usually means
Grey structure onlyFrom about ₹700Foundation, RCC frame, brickwork. No plaster, flooring, doors, windows, electrical or plumbing
Economy₹1,450 – ₹1,750Standard brands, basic vitrified or ceramic flooring, basic fittings
Standard₹1,900 – ₹2,300Branded cement and Fe500D steel, better flooring and sanitaryware, concealed wiring
Premium₹2,900 – ₹3,500+Premium finishes, larger spans, upgraded windows, doors and fittings
Luxury₹4,000+Imported finishes, extensive interiors and services

Use these as a starting point for budgeting, not as a quote. The real number for your plot comes from a BOQ based on your drawings.

What the Rate Does Not Include

  • Land cost, stamp duty and registration
  • Building plan sanction and authority fees (see our guide to the NKDA sanction process)
  • Soil investigation and structural design fees
  • Electricity, water and sewer connection charges
  • Modular kitchen, wardrobes and most interior work
  • Loan interest during construction

That lines up with the grey-structure figure above. Labour, shuttering, plumbing, electrical, flooring, doors, windows, paint and fittings take a standard build the rest of the way to around ₹2,000 per sq ft. Current material prices are on our Kolkata material rates page.

What Pushes Cost Up in Kolkata and New Town

  • Pile foundations. Much of eastern Kolkata sits on soft, low-lying or filled ground. When the soil report calls for piles, foundation cost rises sharply compared with isolated footings. Read why.
  • Steel consumption. G+4 buildings, larger column-free spaces and cantilevers use more steel per sq ft than the thumb rule.
  • Monsoon. Four months of heavy rain slow excavation, masonry and plaster. Tight schedules that ignore the monsoon usually end in extra labour cost.
  • Specification drift. Changing tiles, fittings or layouts after work starts is the most common source of budget overrun we see.

How to Check a Contractor’s Per Sq Ft Quote

  1. Ask for the specification sheet behind the rate: cement and steel brands and grades, brick type, flooring, doors, windows, fittings.
  2. Ask whether the rate is on built-up area or carpet area, and whether staircase, lift well and terrace are counted.
  3. Get a BOQ from your drawings and compare quotes item by item.
  4. Agree how extra items and rate changes will be priced before signing.
  5. Have running bills verified against measured work on site. This is where most overbilling happens. See BOQ vs. estimation.

Common Questions

What is the construction cost per sq ft in Kolkata in 2026?

Published 2026 estimates put a standard-specification residential building in Kolkata at roughly Rs 1,900 to Rs 2,300 per sq ft of built-up area, economy builds at about Rs 1,450 to Rs 1,750, and premium builds from about Rs 2,900 upward. Land, approvals and interiors are extra.

Does the per sq ft rate include land and approvals?

No. A per sq ft construction rate covers building work only. Land, stamp duty and registration, building plan sanction fees, utility connections, loan interest and most interiors are separate.

Why do two quotes for the same house differ so much?

Usually because they assume different specifications: cement and steel brand, foundation type, brick or block, window and door material, flooring, fittings and finishes. Compare quotes line by line against a BOQ, not by the headline per sq ft figure.
